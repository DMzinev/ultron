Sessions 1 & 2: Costing & FIFO

  • Unit Cost: Total Cost / Volume
  • Equivalent Units (FIFO): completed + closing WIP EU - opening WIP EU
  • Cost per EU: Current Costs Added / Equivalent Units of work done
  • Predetermined OH Rate: Budgeted OH Cost / Budgeted Allocation Base

Sessions 3, 4 & 5: CVP, ABC & Variance

  • CM per unit: Price - Variable Cost per unit
  • Breakeven (units): Fixed Costs / CM per unit
  • CM per bottleneck unit: CM per unit × units per bottleneck unit
  • DM Price Variance: $(Standard Price - Actual Price) \times Actual Quantity$
  • DM Efficiency Variance: $(Standard Qty allowed - Actual Qty used) \times Standard Price$
